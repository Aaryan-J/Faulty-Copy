*__FAULTY COPY__* is a tense, minimalist psychological deduction thriller built from scratch for the Brackeys Game Jam 2026.2. 

Find the itch.io page on https://myxsss.itch.io/faulty-copy

As a bunker security agent at Outpost Omega, your task is cold, clinical, and simple: examine the biometric data of identical personnel duplicates sitting across your desk. Cross-reference their vital readings against verified human medical baselines. Identify the physiological flaws of the extraterrestrial mimics and purge the duplicates.

But be warned... the entities are adapting, their anomalies are fading into near-invisible numbers ( and the ultimate threat might be closer than your instruments suggest).

# INTERFACING COMMANDS 

    MOUSE CLICK: Select a profile portrait sheet to immediately fire your sidearm and eliminate the target copy.
    SPACEBAR: Fast-forward or skip cinematic story transmissions during system lockdowns.
     Be sure to click on the "READ PROTOCOL" button to check the usual readings you want... and the unusual readings you should look out for.
